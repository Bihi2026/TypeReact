# What you can do on TypeReact

TypeReact is a place to discuss public media with **evidence on the record**. You respond to videos, posts, podcasts, and other sources with **Reactions**, open **accountability cases** when something needs sustained scrutiny, and engage **creators** who claim their profiles. A parallel **Stories** space lets you read and write fiction. The **Learning Center** and **policies** explain how good discussion works here.

You need an account for creating content, messaging, saving, following, and most personal features. Anyone can browse much of the public discussion.

---

## Getting around

- **Main menu** — Home, Explore, Reactions, Accountability Cases, Stories, Learning Center, Topics, Countries, and Creators.
- **Your menu** — Following, Saved, Research Circles, Become a Writer, Messages, Notifications, and Profile.
- **Theme** — Switch between light, dark, or match your device.
- **Quick search (command menu)** — Jump to major pages, look up a Reaction by its ID, or browse Reactions, cases, stories, creators, and topics without hunting through menus.
- **Sign in / Sign up** — Create an account or sign in to unlock personal features.

## Discover what’s being discussed

### Country scope

On Home and Explore you can choose a **country** (or **All countries**) so feeds and lists match that region. If you’re not signed in, the default is everything worldwide. Signed-in members can also set a default country in Settings so local discussion is preferred.

### Home

Your starting overview of the conversation:

- **Under Discussion** — Sources that are drawing the most analysis in your selected country (or worldwide).
- **Trending Reactions** — Highly upvoted evidence-based responses in that same scope.
- **Your Feeds** — **Local** (your country) and **Global** (newest everywhere).
- **Trending Topics** — Subjects where discussion is concentrating, with Reaction and case activity.
- **Search** — From the home experience you can search into claims, creators, and Reaction IDs.

### Explore

A broader browse with the same country filter:

- Topic chips to jump into subject areas.
- Tabs for **Sources**, **Reactions**, and **Cases** in that country (or all countries).

### Search

Find discussion by keywords or identifiers. You can filter (for example by creator, author, topic, source type, evidence level, or date) and see results across Reactions, cases, and creators. If you know a Reaction ID (like `TR-…`), you can jump straight to it.

### Topics

A directory of discussion subjects (accountability-style categories such as integrity and conduct themes). Open a topic to see Reactions tagged with it.

### Countries

A directory of places. Open a country to see local discussion and activity tied to that region.

## Reactions

A **Reaction** is your evidence-based response to a public source—for example a YouTube video, TikTok, podcast episode, or article. You choose a stance, write an analysis, attach evidence, and publish a durable record others can cite, challenge, and reply to.

### Create a Reaction

Creating is a guided multi-step flow:

- **Choose what you’re doing** — Respond to a specific source (a Reaction), or write a broader **creator review** of someone’s body of work.
- **Paste a source URL** — TypeReact analyzes supported links (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, podcasts, articles, books, and similar) to pull title, thumbnail, platform, and creator.
- **Confirm the source** — Check the detected details (thumbnail, platform, category, language, date, creator) before continuing, or go back and try another URL.
- **Confirm the creator** — Affirm who made the content, or search by handle and platform if detection is wrong. Publishing can create an **unclaimed** creator profile when needed. You’ll see whether that creator already has a TypeReact presence and how much discussion already exists on that source.
- **Pick a stance** — **Agree**, **Disagree**, **Mixed**, or **Unpack**. The stance frames how readers understand your piece.
- **Write the Reaction** — Add a title and body with formatting (bold, italic, headings, quotes, lists, links), plus Write and Preview modes.
- **Mention people** — Use @mentions while writing so the right people are notified.
- **Tag topics** — Add up to five topic tags so the Reaction appears on topic pages.
- **Quote another Reaction** — Optionally cite another Reaction by its ID (or arrive from a “Quote” action) so your piece builds on prior work.
- **Attach evidence** — Add links, screenshots, documents/PDFs, videos, timestamps, or research references (with uploads where allowed). You can remove items before publishing.
- **Visibility** — Publish **Public**, limit to **Followers only**, or **Save as draft**.
- **Drafts** — Resume or discard an in-progress draft; open Create with an existing draft to keep editing and publish later.
- **Publish preview** — Review stance, ID, title, body, evidence, and any quoted Reaction before it goes live.

### Find and browse Reactions

- **Reactions list** — Newest public Reactions, filterable by stance (All / Agree / Disagree / Mixed / Unpack), with load more.
- **Reaction cards** — Each card shows stance, Reaction ID, draft badge if relevant, source/creator line, time, title, excerpt, author, evidence rating, reply count, and quick like / save / follow when you’re signed in.
- **Home & Explore** — Trending and country-scoped lists as described above.
- **Following** — Signed-in feed of new Reactions from authors and creators you follow.
- **Search, Topics, Countries** — Discovery surfaces that surface matching Reactions.
- **Creator profiles** — Reactions about that creator’s content.
- **Member profiles** — Published Reactions; on your own profile, **Drafts** and **Saved** as well.
- **Saved library** — Dedicated place for bookmarked Reactions (see Saved below).

### Open a Reaction

On a Reaction page you can:

- **See identity at a glance** — Stance badge, Reaction ID, evidence rating, title, topic chips (authors can adjust topics when live), author, published/updated dates, and view count.
- **Record a view** — Opening a live Reaction contributes to the public view counter.
- **“Since you last visited”** — When you return signed in, a short digest can highlight what changed since your last visit.
- **Quoted Reaction** — If this piece quotes another, a preview card links to it.
- **Source panel (“Responding to”)** — See the original source thumbnail and metadata; **Watch in TypeReact** when the video can play in-app; open the creator’s profile when claimed; optionally **save the source** to your library.
- **Reading comfort** — Adjust text size for the analysis.
- **Read the analysis** — Rich text with headings, quotes, lists, mentions, and evidence embedded in the body. You can copy a permalink to a block or deep-link to a highlighted section.
- **Ask “Source?” on claims** — Signed-in readers can request sources on claim blocks; the author is notified.
- **Amend (author)** — Authors can publish a new version with a short “what changed” note.
- **Version history** — Readers can review numbered versions and change notes when amendments exist.
- **Claim map** — Structured claims marked Supported / Disputed / Unverified, linked to evidence; authors can maintain the map.
- **Evidence panel** — Filter All / Documents / Screenshots / Videos / Links / Timestamps; open or preview items; **attest** or **challenge** evidence; copy a link to a specific item.
- **Source debate graph** — See how stances break down and jump to other Reactions on the **same source URL**.
- **Creator snapshot** — Name, handle, claimed/unclaimed status, followers, Reactions received, and a link to the creator profile.
- **Cite this Reaction** — Copy citation text, grab an embed snippet, or use a QR code for classrooms and articles.

### Engage with a Reaction

- **Like** — Show support; the count updates for everyone.
- **Save** — Bookmark into your library, optionally into a **collection**, with an optional **research note**. You can update that later.
- **Share** — Share to major networks, use your device’s share sheet, or copy the link, deep link, Reaction ID, or citation.
- **Follow the author** — Their future Reactions show up in your Following feed.
- **Mute the author** — Hide their Reactions from your feeds.
- **Message** — Start a direct conversation about this Reaction (not shown for your own posts).
- **Quote** — Jump into Create with this Reaction prefilled as the quoted piece.
- **Reply chain** — Threaded discussion (with nesting), reply counts, and sign-in when needed. Compose with @mentions, emoji, **stickers**, and **voice notes**. Sort by newest, most evidenced, or original poster first; load more; report individual replies.
- **Community notes** — Context notes separate from replies; others can vote Helpful / Not helpful.
- **Promote to case (author)** — Turn the Reaction into an accountability case when the evidence warrants sustained scrutiny (requires evidence). Later you can open the linked case from the Reaction.

### Evidence tools (in depth)

- **Evidence rating** — A 0–100 score (with a plain-language label) summarizing how well the piece is sourced, shown on cards and on the page.
- **Typed gallery** — Browse attached items by type, with titles, descriptions, timestamps, verification cues, and who added them.
- **Attest or challenge** — Signed-in readers vote confidence on each evidence item.
- **Evidence requests** — Open “Source?” asks appear in a panel; authors can **Resolve** or **Dismiss**; recent outcomes stay visible.

### Official dialogue with creators

- **Official responses** — When a named/claimed creator replies on the record, their turns appear in a dedicated dialogue (separate from ordinary comments).
- **Author rebuttal** — The Reaction author can answer on the record after a creator turn (dialogue length is capped).
- **Arrival cue** — You may get a sound cue when an official response appears, depending on your notification preferences.
- **Creator inbox** — Creators see Reactions that name them and can jump in to respond.

### Safety on Reactions

- **Report a Reaction** — Flag with a category and details (sign-in required to submit).
- **Report a reply** — Same idea from inside the reply chain.

## Accountability cases

**Cases** are for issues that need a clearer public record over time—misinformation, harassment, scams, and similar problems—not just a single Reaction.

### Create a case

A wizard walks you through choosing the **creator** involved, a **category**, building **claims** and **evidence**, then reviewing before you open the case. You can also start a case from a creator’s profile or by promoting a Reaction.

### Browse and open cases

- Browse cases and filter by status (for example open, under review, responded, resolved).
- Open a case file to see claims, evidence, strengths and weaknesses, contradictions, missing evidence, community notes, a **timeline**, and **version history** when relevant.
- Adjust **reading text size** for comfort.
- See a **visit/update digest** when you return to a case you’ve viewed before.

### Engage with a case

- **Follow** — Stay updated on the case.
- **Save** — Keep it in your research library.
- **Share or report** — Spread the record or flag rule-breaking content.
- **Message** — Start a conversation in the context of the case.
- **Community notes** — Add contextual notes for other readers.
- **Official creator response** — Claimed creators can respond on the record when the case is about them.

## Creators

**Creators** are public figures and publishers whose content is discussed on TypeReact. Profiles can be unclaimed (community-linked) or claimed and verified by the person who owns the channels.

### Directory

- Browse all creators with search and filters (topic, platform, claim/verification status) and sorting (followers, Reactions, response rate, name).
- See **most discussed** creators by Reaction volume.
- Open cards that show bio highlights, platforms, follower and Reaction stats, and claim status.

### Creator profile

- See bio, platforms, topics, country, and whether the profile is active on TypeReact or still unclaimed.
- Follow the creator; message them; open a case; report the profile.
- Browse tabs for indexed library content, **Reactions about them**, and related **cases**.
- Review a **profile snapshot** (followers, sources, Reactions received, official responses, open cases, response rate).
- Use official links and local country context when available.
- **Claim this profile** when you’re eligible (or apply via Become a Creator).

### Apply or claim

Creators verify channel ownership through an application: select platforms and links, prove ownership (for example a one-time code), provide public identity and private verification details, then submit for review. After approval you get a verified badge and can post **official responses**.

### Creator reviews

Separate from a single-source Reaction, a **creator review** assesses someone’s overall work. You create it from Create, and others can read the published review on its own page.

## Profiles and following

### Profiles

- View your profile or another member’s (private profiles stay private).
- See tabs such as Reactions and cases; on your own profile also **inbox**, **saved**, and **drafts**.
- Follow a reactor from their profile.
- Edit how you appear; share your profile; see an **evidence-score** style snapshot of your habits.
- Jump to a claimed **creator** or **writer** identity when linked.

### Following

- A signed-in feed of Reactions from researchers and creators you follow.
- Side lists of people you follow, with shortcuts to discover more if the list is empty.
- Load more as the feed grows.

## Research Circles

**Research Circles** are private groups for collaborating on a case or topic.

- See circles you belong to; **create** one anchored to a case or topic.
- **Accept or decline** invites.
- Inside a circle: post (with mentions and attachments), edit or delete your posts.
- **Invite** people by username; cancel pending invites.
- Edit the circle’s name and description.
- Leave a circle; owners can remove members, transfer ownership, or delete the circle.

---

## Messages

- Inbox of conversations tied to a **Reaction**, **case**, **creator**, or a direct thread.
- Open a thread, send text, and attach files.
- Start a message from a Reaction, case, or creator page.
- See unread indicators and read state so you know what’s new.

---

## Notifications

- Inbox with an unread count.
- Filter by type—replies, mentions, new followers, following activity, creator responses, evidence activity, verification, messages, circles, and similar.
- Mark one notification or all as read.
- Jump straight to the related content.
- In **Settings → Notifications**, turn categories (and digests) on or off so you only hear about what matters.

---

## Saved library

Your personal research shelf:

- Save **Reactions**, **cases**, and **sources**.
- Organize into **collections**; create or delete collections; move items between them.
- Attach **research notes** to saved items.
- Unsave or remove items when you’re done.

## Stories

A reading and writing track alongside discussion.

### For readers

- Discover stories with search, genres, status, tags, and options to hide mature content.
- Browse featured, trending, rising, and completed work; open genre pages.
- Read **chapter by chapter**; **continue reading** where you left off (progress can be remembered on your device).
- Vote, follow writers, add stories to reading lists, comment, and use listen/read-aloud where available.
- Keep a **library** of in-progress reads.

### For writers

- **Become a Writer** — Apply with a profile and writing sample; wait for approval.
- **Writer dashboard** — Start stories, edit chapters, manage covers and settings, track status, and see stats and alerts.
- **Contests** — Browse contests and enter with a published story; judging is handled blindly by the platform team.

## Settings

Signed-in members can manage:

- **Profile** — Display name, username, bio, website, and country shown on your account.
- **Privacy** — Whether your profile is public, whether country shows, search visibility, who can message you, and activity status.
- **Mutes** — Authors and topics you’ve muted so they stay out of your feeds.
- **Notifications** — Per-category alerts and digests.
- **Reading** — Default text size / reading comfort.
- **Country** — Default country for local feed preference and regional trends.

---

## For platform staff (admin)

People with staff access can:

- Review **platform overview** stats.
- Work an **inbox** of reports and moderation activity.
- Manage **people** — users, creators, verification queues (approve/reject), and writer applications.
- Manage **content** — case evidence review, flagged stories, contests (including blind judging), and Learning Center resources.
- Use **system** tools such as account sync for the auth provider.

Ordinary members do not see this area.
